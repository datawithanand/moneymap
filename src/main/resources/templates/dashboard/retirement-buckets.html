Retirement Bucket Planner

Freefincal-style 3-bucket drawdown: split your retirement corpus by how soon you'll need it, rather than holding it all in one blended allocation. Bucket 1 (years 1-3) stays in cash/liquid funds so a market downturn never forces you to sell equity at a loss to pay bills. Bucket 2 (years 4-7) sits in short-duration debt. Bucket 3 (year 8+) stays invested for growth and periodically refills buckets 1 and 2.

Pre-filled from your PF/PPF/NPS/Gratuity/Government Schemes total.

Suggested Buckets

Bucket 1 — Cash / Liquid (Yrs 1-3)

Bucket 2 — Short Debt (Yrs 4-7)

Bucket 3 — Growth (Yr 8+)

Buckets 1 and 2 together cover less than 7 years of expenses — your corpus may be too small to fully fund this drawdown plan without Bucket 3 running down faster than intended. Consider revisiting your retirement readiness or expense assumptions.

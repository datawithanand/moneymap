Import from CAS Statement

Upload your CAMS/KFintech Consolidated Account Statement (CAS) PDF to bulk-import your mutual fund holdings. Request your own CAS PDF directly from camsonline.com or kfintech.com — MoneyMap never contacts them on your behalf. The file and password are used only to extract the text in-memory for this one request; nothing is written to disk, and nothing is sent anywhere else. You'll review every detected holding before anything is saved.

CAS PDFs are usually protected with your PAN (uppercase) and date of birth (DDMMYYYY), or similar — check the email that delivered it.

This parser targets the commonly-observed CAS layout, which isn't officially standardized — extraction accuracy is best-effort. The next screen lets you edit or exclude any row before saving, so nothing incorrect gets imported silently.

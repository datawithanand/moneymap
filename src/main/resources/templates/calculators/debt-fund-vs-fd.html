Debt Mutual Fund vs Fixed Deposit

Under current Indian tax law (post 1 Apr 2023), debt mutual fund gains are taxed at your slab rate at redemption — the same rate as FD interest — so there's no automatic tax advantage anymore for new investments. The debt fund's edge here is purely tax deferral: FD interest is taxed every year on accrual, while a debt fund is only taxed once, when you actually redeem it. If your fund units were bought before 1 Apr 2023, they may still be grandfathered into the old indexation-based 20% LTCG treatment — tick the box below if that applies to you.
